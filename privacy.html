Privacy Policy

Streakboard · Last updated October 2026

Streakboard is a habit tracker and daily planner. This policy explains what information the app handles when you use it, where that information is kept, and what control you have over it.

Information in your account

To sign in, Streakboard needs your email address. If you choose Google sign-in, Google shares your email, name and profile picture with the sign-in service. Streakboard only uses the email to identify your account. If you sign up with a password, the login service keeps it in a protected form that the app owner cannot read.

Everything you add inside the app is saved to your account so it appears on all your devices:

  • your habits and the days you mark as done
  • mood and sleep entries
  • to-do plans and their due dates
  • feedback you send, along with your browser and device type so problems can be reproduced

How it is used

This information is used to run the app: signing you in, saving and syncing your data, showing your statistics, and answering feedback. It is not used for advertising or marketing.

Where it is kept

Account details and saved entries are stored with Supabase, which provides the database and sign-in. The website is hosted on Cloudflare. When you open the page, your browser also fetches fonts from Google Fonts and a code library from the jsDelivr network, so those services can see that a request was made.

Streakboard also keeps a copy on your own device. This holds your sign-in session, the month you are viewing, changes made while offline (sent when you reconnect) and your light or dark preference. You can remove it by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Sharing

Streakboard does not sell your information, does not show ads, and does not run analytics or tracking tools. The services named above handle data only to keep the app running. Other users cannot see your habits or plans.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete your habits and plans at any time inside the app.
  • To have your whole account and everything saved in it deleted, send a request through the Feedback button in the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.
